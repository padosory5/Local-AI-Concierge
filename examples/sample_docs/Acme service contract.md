# Service Agreement between Acme Supplies Ltd. and Brightside Studio

This agreement was signed on 12 March 2026 by Dana Kim, Operations Lead at Acme Supplies Ltd., and by Jun Park for Brightside Studio.

## Services
Brightside Studio will design and maintain Acme's online catalogue, including monthly updates of product photos.

## Payment terms
Acme pays a monthly fee of 2,400 dollars. Invoices are due within 30 days of the invoice date. Late payments carry a fee of 1.5 percent per month.

## Termination
Either party may end this agreement with 60 days' written notice. If Acme ends the agreement within the first six months, a one-time early termination penalty of 3,000 dollars applies.
